sorting, or the mean of the two middle scores for an even count), and print it after each
student's average and grade, as below. After the students, print the name of the student with
the best average. Add a test for `median` next to the existing test, and make sure
`lyth test` passes.

```
ada: 91.7 A, median 92.0
bob: 73.3 C, median 75.0
cyd: 65.3 F, median 66.0
dee: 85.0 B, median 85.0
best: ada
```
